Privacy policy

Coding Adventure Junior is published by an individual developer operating as OMSW Studio. Contact: omswlab.support@gmail.com.

Coding Adventure Junior is a learning game for children. World 1 can be played without creating an account. Guest progress stays on the device and may be imported into a parent account after confirmation.

Supabase Auth processes the parent email, password and authentication information for account management and password reset. Online sync stores the account identifier, completed levels and missions. The legacy Jin code (formerly World 2) also supports quiz answers, exercises, study reports and a device identifier for synchronization, although Jin’s world is not generally available in this build.

World 1 stars, badges, trophies and reports are derived from progress. Optional learner nickname and age are stored locally per account. The certificate name is used to generate the certificate at the time of entry. Sound and language preferences stay on the device. Exported or shared PNG/PDF files may persist on the device or in the service selected by the parent.

Supabase provides account and database services for authentication and learning sync. Existing order, membership and legacy payment-provider records may remain. Account and progress data are needed to deliver the service until deletion is requested or they are no longer necessary. Limited records may require retention for legal obligations, fraud prevention or dispute resolution. Actual retention periods by category, backups, hosting regions, transfers and all service providers must be verified and disclosed before public release. Signing out does not delete an account.

This policy site contains no data-entry forms or tracking scripts added by us. If hosted on GitHub Pages, the hosting provider may log IP addresses for security. When you send email, email providers process the message and sender information to deliver and manage the request.

Children’s privacy

Accounts and emails should belong to parents or guardians. Children should not enter their own email, address, telephone number, school or other unnecessary personal information, including a real name in the nickname field.

Parents can review progress, clear the local learner profile and manage the account. The arithmetic parental gate is intended to prevent accidental access, not to establish legally sufficient parental consent.

Parents and guardians may contact omswlab.support@gmail.com to request access, correction, deletion, withdrawal of consent, objection or other applicable rights. The developer must verify the requester’s authority before acting. Direct parental notice and verifiable parental consent where legally required must be implemented and checked before collecting children’s personal information online in a public release.

Contact the developer

Email omswlab.support@gmail.com from the address registered to the parent account, if possible. If you cannot access it, request an alternative verification method. Identify Coding Adventure Junior and specify account and associated data deletion or the privacy right you wish to exercise. Do not send passwords, OTPs, identity numbers, card details or children’s personal information. The operator must monitor the mailbox, verify authority, and communicate next steps and the outcome.
