Privacy policy

Hopnib Code is published by an individual developer operating as OMSW Studio. Contact: omswlab.support@gmail.com.

Hopnib Code is a learning game for children. World 1 can be played without creating an account. Guest progress stays on the device and may be imported into a parent account after confirmation.

Supabase Auth processes the parent email, password and authentication information for account management and password reset. Online sync stores the account identifier, completed levels and missions. The app also contains legacy learning-sync code for unreleased worlds, including answers, exercises, learning reports and synchronization identifiers. These worlds are not available for general play in this version.

World 1 stars, badges, trophies and reports are derived from progress. Optional learner nickname and age are stored locally per account. The certificate name is used to generate the certificate at the time of entry. Sound and language preferences stay on the device. Exported or shared PNG/PDF files may persist on the device or in the service selected by the parent.

Supabase provides authentication and learning-data storage; the current project is hosted in Singapore. Existing test purchase, entitlement and legacy payment records may remain. Google Play and RevenueCat were used for internal purchase testing; new purchases and the RevenueCat purchase SDK are disabled in this closed-testing build. Account and learning data are kept while necessary to provide the service or until deletion is requested, subject to necessary legal, fraud prevention and dispute-resolution retention. Data may be processed outside your country. Contact us for applicable retention details and deletion requests. Signing out does not delete an account.

This policy site contains no data-entry forms or tracking scripts added by us. If hosted on GitHub Pages, the hosting provider may log IP addresses for security. When you send email, email providers process the message and sender information to deliver and manage the request.

Children’s privacy

Accounts and emails should belong to parents or guardians. Children should not enter their own email, address, telephone number, school or other unnecessary personal information, including a real name in the nickname field.

Parents can review progress, clear the local learner profile and manage the account. The arithmetic parental gate is intended to prevent accidental access, not to establish legally sufficient parental consent.

Parents and guardians may contact omswlab.support@gmail.com to request access, correction, deletion, withdrawal of consent, objection or other applicable rights. We verify the requester’s authority before acting. Do not submit children’s identifying information. Where law requires direct parental notice and verifiable consent before online collection of children’s personal information, those requirements must be satisfied before collecting such information.

Contact the developer

Email omswlab.support@gmail.com from the address registered to your parent account, if possible. If you cannot access it, request an alternative verification method. Identify Hopnib Code and specify your request. Do not send passwords, OTPs, identity numbers, card details or children’s personal information. We will verify your authority and communicate next steps and the outcome.
