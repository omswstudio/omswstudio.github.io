Account and data deletion

For eligible free accounts: open Parent account → Privacy and account → Delete account permanently. Read the warning, type DELETE and confirm the current password. The app must confirm deletion before signing out.

V160.2 cannot automatically delete accounts with purchase or membership history. All parents, including paying members, may request account and associated data deletion by email without reinstalling the app. The developer must verify authority, handle any relevant entitlement or payment obligations, and actually delete the associated data rather than merely disable the account. Any legitimate retention exception must be explained, including the categories, purpose and applicable retention period.

Previously exported or shared PNG/PDF files, copies on other devices and separate guest data are not automatically removed. Manage those copies separately. Sending a request is not confirmation of deletion; the developer must communicate the outcome after processing.

Contact the developer

Email omswlab.support@gmail.com from the address registered to the parent account, if possible. If you cannot access it, request an alternative verification method. Identify Coding Adventure Junior and specify account and associated data deletion or the privacy right you wish to exercise. Do not send passwords, OTPs, identity numbers, card details or children’s personal information. The operator must monitor the mailbox, verify authority, and communicate next steps and the outcome.
