Account and data deletion

For eligible free accounts: open Parent account → Privacy and account → Delete account permanently. Read the warning, type DELETE and confirm the current password. The app must confirm deletion before signing out.

Accounts with purchase or membership history may require developer review before deletion. All parents, including those with payment history, may request account and associated data deletion by email without reinstalling the app. We will verify authority, address applicable payment obligations and delete associated data, not merely disable the account. If limited records must be retained for legitimate reasons, we will explain the categories, purpose and applicable retention period.

Previously exported or shared PNG/PDF files, copies on other devices and separate guest data are not automatically removed. Manage those copies separately. Sending a request is not confirmation of deletion; the developer must communicate the outcome after processing.

Contact the developer

Email omswlab.support@gmail.com from the address registered to your parent account, if possible. If you cannot access it, request an alternative verification method. Identify Hopnib Code and specify your request. Do not send passwords, OTPs, identity numbers, card details or children’s personal information. We will verify your authority and communicate next steps and the outcome.
